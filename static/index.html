      Evidence, QTL labels and coordinate provenance

      Reported P-values are preserved. Orange diamonds identify values verified as FDR-adjusted in selected original tables; grey circles have unverified adjustment status. These statistics should not be interpreted as uniformly calibrated.

      GWAS Atlas QTL names are shown on their original GWAS rows. A QTL label alone does not establish a separate linkage-QTL study; no independently identified linkage-QTL dataset is loaded.

      Source-labelled IRGSP-1.0 coordinates are used. Historical coordinate remapping by the source has not been independently revalidated. Exact duplicate evidence rows are grouped by default; records may still share samples and are not independent replications.
